Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Lemnos Street Indoor Sports Pty Ltd (ABN 21 105 503 526), trading as Cricket Revolution, ("we", "us", "our") collects, holds, uses and discloses personal information through the Krickora booking platform (the "Service") at cricketrevolution.com.au and the installed app version of it.

We manage personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). We keep this policy up to date and will post any changes here with a new "last updated" date.

The kinds of personal information we collect and hold

When you create an account: first name, last name, email address, mobile number, password (stored as a one-way hash — we never see or store your actual password), postcode/suburb, and how you heard about us.

When you use the Service: booking details (date, time, lane, duration, price, discount codes used, account credit applied), payment records (amount, Stripe payment/session references — we do not store your full card number; card details are collected and processed directly by Stripe), a temporary per-booking door access code, waitlist entries, and any notes attached to a fault report.

Athlete/coaching information: where a parent or guardian links a child (an "athlete") to their account for coaching, we hold the athlete's first name, last name, and which coach(es) they're linked to, plus their coaching session history. This information is created and controlled by the parent/guardian account — see "Children's information" below.

Coach information: for users registered as coaches, we additionally hold coaching tier, pricing, booking statements and account balance.

Communications data: if you contact us, or if we send you a booking confirmation, reminder, or receipt, we hold a record of that message and, for email, delivery status (sent/delivered/opened/clicked/ bounced) so we can tell if an important message like a door code failed to reach you.

Push notifications: if you enable push notifications, we hold the technical subscription details needed to deliver them (via your browser/device's push service) and your notification preferences (which categories you've turned on or off).

Usage information: when you use the Service we automatically collect limited technical and usage data — pages viewed, device/browser type, and similar session information — to keep the Service working and to understand how it's used. This data does not include payment details and is retained only for a limited period (currently no longer than around six months) before being deleted.

Referral/mate information: if you invite someone to a booking ("add a mate"), we use the mobile number or email you provide to identify and invite them — this is used only to complete that invitation.

Marketing preference: whether you've opted in to promotional communications, separate from the service messages (like booking confirmations and door codes) that we need to send you to operate your booking.

We do not collect government identifiers (e.g. Medicare, driver's licence, tax file numbers) and we do not collect health, biometric or other "sensitive information" as defined by the Privacy Act.

How we collect personal information

Directly from you: when you register, make a booking, link an athlete, contact us, or use the app. Automatically: limited technical/usage data as described above. From a payment processor: Stripe provides us confirmation that a payment succeeded and a reference number — never your full card details. If you sign in with a third-party account (e.g. Google), that provider shares the name and email you've authorised it to share; we don't request anything beyond that.

Why we collect, use and disclose your personal information

We collect and use personal information to:

  • create and manage your account and any linked athlete profiles;
  • process bookings, payments, refunds and account credit;
  • generate and manage the temporary door access code for your booking, and operate the facility's automated lighting/entry systems for the duration of your session;
  • send you service communications — booking confirmations, reminders, door codes, receipts, and operational updates (these are not "marketing" and you cannot opt out of them while retaining an active booking, since they're necessary to deliver the service you've booked);
  • send you promotional/marketing communications, but only where you've opted in, and you can withdraw that consent at any time via your account settings or the unsubscribe link in any marketing message, without needing to log in or provide extra information;
  • respond to enquiries, fault reports and complaints;
  • detect and prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorised access (for example, rate-limiting repeated login or booking attempts);
  • understand how the Service is used so we can improve it; and
  • comply with our legal obligations.

We do not use your personal information to make any automated decision that has a legal or otherwise significant effect on you. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share personal information with the service providers we use to run Krickora, each only for the purpose of providing their part of the service, and each bound by their own security and privacy commitments:

We may also disclose personal information where required or authorised by law — for example, to respond to a lawful request from a regulator or law enforcement.

We do not disclose personal information to any other third party for their own marketing purposes, and we do not sell personal information to anyone.

Overseas disclosure

Some of the providers listed above (including Stripe, Convex, Vercel and Resend) are based in, or process data in, the United States, and personal information may be held or processed there as a result. Where practicable, an entity we disclose personal information to overseas takes reasonable steps to protect it consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles; however, an overseas recipient may not itself be directly subject to the Privacy Act, and in some circumstances you may not be able to seek redress under Australian law against an overseas recipient directly. We remain accountable for personal information we disclose overseas in accordance with APP 8. By using the Service, you acknowledge this disclosure occurs as described in this policy.

Children's information

Krickora is used by adults booking sessions for themselves, and by parents/guardians who create and manage "athlete" profiles for their children to be coached. We handle children's information as follows:

  • A child's profile can only be created and controlled by a parent or guardian's own account — a child does not create their own separate login.
  • Coaches only see athlete information for sessions they are actually allocated to coach, not a facility-wide roster.
  • We do not send marketing communications directly to a child — all communications relating to a linked athlete go to the parent/guardian account.
  • A parent or guardian can view, correct or request deletion of their child's information at any time by contacting us or through their account.
  • We collect only the information needed to run a coaching session for a child — name and coach linkage/session history. We do not collect a child's date of birth, school, or other information beyond what coaching allocation requires.
  • We are monitoring the OAIC's forthcoming Children's Online Privacy Code (in draft, expected to be registered by 10 December 2026) and will review and, if needed, update these practices once it is finalised.

If you believe a child's information has been provided to us without appropriate parental or guardian consent, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

Cookies and similar technologies

The Service uses browser local storage (not third-party advertising cookies) to keep you signed in and to make the app work offline as an installed PWA. We use limited first-party analytics to understand usage of the Service, described above. We do not use third-party advertising trackers, and we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information we hold from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure, including:

  • encrypting data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) between your device and our systems;
  • storing passwords only as one-way cryptographic hashes, never in plain text;
  • never storing your full payment card details ourselves — that's handled directly by Stripe, a PCI-DSS-compliant payment processor;
  • rate-limiting and monitoring to detect and slow abusive or unauthorised access attempts; and
  • restricting administrative access to the Service to authorised staff, with additional verification required for sensitive administrative actions.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a data breach that is likely to result in serious harm, we will notify affected individuals and, where required, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC), in accordance with the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

How long we keep your information

We keep personal information only for as long as it's needed for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law (for example, financial record-keeping obligations). Technical/usage data is kept for a limited period, generally no more than around six months, before being deleted. If you close your account, we will delete or de-identify your personal information within a reasonable time, except where we're required to retain it (for example, transaction records relevant to tax or financial reporting obligations, or information relevant to a dispute).

Accessing and correcting your information

You can access and update most of your personal information at any time by logging into your account. If you'd like a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or believe it's inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant or misleading, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time (normally within 30 days), free of charge in most circumstances. In some limited circumstances permitted by law we may decline a request, and if so we'll explain why and how you can complain about that decision.

How to make a complaint

If you have a concern about how we've handled your personal information, please contact us first at admin@revolutionsports.com.au with details of your complaint. We will acknowledge your complaint and aim to resolve it within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner:

  • Website: oaic.gov.au
  • Phone: 1300 363 992

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or the law (including the forthcoming Children's Online Privacy Code). We'll post the updated policy here with a new "last updated" date. If a change is significant, we'll take reasonable steps to bring it to your attention — for example, via an in-app notice.
